Set Up, Design and Care for Vienna Venue: Masa Sallai    







The project H0pe XMAS SHOW, is an XMAS-themed Open Call Exhibition happening at two venues in two citites simultaniously. 
At Universtiy of Applied Arts Vienna we showed Xmas tree hangers, while Student-Exhibitionspace Kunstakademie Düsseldorf showed their matching trees. 
We separeted the famous couple of the holy night and connected two art universities. We crashed a symbol, we tackled a holy day, we celebrated the two as they are, without each other.
Each show was livestreamed and projected into the other space and our founder was reading our manifest while playing cool and modern music.
