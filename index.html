H0PE ART SERVICE

H0pe is a curatorial project initiated by Marcus Wagner in Newcastle Upon Tyne in 2024, with the wish to create his own curatorial structure and therefore freedom to present works in an autonomous way. H0pe organizes itself with regular open calls inviting everybody to submit. The program focusses on a fun experimental approach to exhibition making and likes to concentrate on the effect of an exhibition more than the single work. Themes are choosen to generate a wide range of submissions and transform a simple topic into a vivid show, guiding back to the viewer and telling something about contemporary art production itself. Everybody is invited to contribute
