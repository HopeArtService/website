H0PE ART SERVICE

H0pe is an artistic innitiative founded with the aim to make light, fun, meaningful and at the same time pointless exhibitions and offer a wide range of people the possibility to be part of it. Inspirations for shows can be objects, words, sentences, symbols, situations and more. The project was innitiated by Marcus Wagner in New Castle Upon Tyne, 2024, as a fun idea, quickly developing into an ongoing regular open call program offering an alternative to the already established curatorial landscapes.
