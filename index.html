H0PE ART SERVICE

H0pe is a curatorial project primarily run by Marcus Wagner. He founded the project in Newcastle Upon Tyne in 2024 with the wish to create his own curatorial structure and therefore freedom to present works. H0pe focusses on fun experimental approach to exhibition making an puts it´s focus more on the effect of the exhibition than the single work. Invited is everbody who can contribute something to a specifici open call topic.
