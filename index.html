H0PE ART SERVICE

H0pe is a curatorial project primarily run by Marcus Wagner. He founded the project in Newcastle Upon Tyne in 2024 with the wish to create his own curatorial structure and therefore freedom to present works. H0pe focusses on a fun experimental approach to exhibition making and puts its focus more on the effect of the exhibition than the single work. Themes are choosen to generate a wide range of submissions and transform a archaic topic into a vivid show telling something about art production itself. Everybody is invited to contribute.
