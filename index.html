H0PE ART SERVICE

Hope is an easy going light hearted art service production institution working with people within and outside the field, hoping for a better world within and outside the arts. It was invented and established by Marcus Wagner on his field trip around New Castle Upon Tyne early 2024. Since then the project tries to nourish the art society with group exhibition based on regular open calls, trying to get new faces and characters to get involved. Inspiration comes from objects, words, the banality of everyday life and the art world itself.
