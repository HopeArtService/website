The project "The Nothing Show" started with the idea to fake generate a topic for an open call group exhibition. 
The exhibition became a three-part project:

Part I: Live Topic Draw  
Part II: Boat Exhibition  
Part III: The Nothing Show FINAL  

The whole concept was strongly driven by a DADA attitude and welcomed randomness. 
The fleeting exhibition first existed on a floating boat island and later returned as a physical exhibition format. 
The curatorial decision to present works like relics of a past event put the attention back to the video — which is the main “Show”.
